Your fish's age, needs and previous care are preserved when you change location. Worldwide weather uses Open-Meteo when internet is available.

Outdoor temperature fallback

When live weather is unavailable, set a fixed outdoor temperature. The normal temperature simulation still runs.

Game preferences

Storage: browser localStorage. Clearing Brave website data, or using private browsing, can erase saved progress. Separate devices do not automatically share saves.

How to take care of your fish

  1. Feeding: Tap the water to drop one pellet. Eating reduces hunger by 0.1. After seven days, the golden minnow becomes a golden sea snake, fed with small living minnows instead.
  2. Playing: Swipe within the tank or toggle the lamp to reduce boredom by 0.1. The fish darts away when touched.
  3. Temperature: Use AirCon or Heater to keep the water safe. Each control can be switched off by tapping it again.

Exact survival rules

  • Both hunger and boredom start at 2.5 and rise 0.1 every 20 minutes.
  • Hunger below 0.2 for one continuous hour: gluttony death. Hunger above 4.7 for one continuous hour: starvation death.
  • Boredom below 0.2: immediate overexcitement death. Boredom above 4.7: immediate boredom death.
  • Water below 14°C continuously for 30 minutes: hypothermia. Above 28°C continuously for 30 minutes: hyperthermia.
  • AirCon cools 1°C per 30 minutes. Heater warms 1.5°C per 30 minutes. With controls off, water returns toward its natural target by 0.2°C per 5 minutes.
  • Food released behind the fish cannot be noticed for three seconds. Unconsumed food sinks and disappears.
  • After death, press and hold the NEW FISH strip for two seconds to start again.
